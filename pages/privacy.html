PAYMENT GATEWAY

  • We don't offer direct checkout — here's why:
  • Every blade we forge is built to be carried, trusted, and passed down — not clicked into a cart and forgotten. Because of that, we don't sell on impulse.
    Before your knife ships, we want to understand exactly what you need it for: hunting, skinning, camp work, everyday carry, your grip, your hand size, even which hand you favor. These aren't small details .They shape blade geometry, handle fit, and edge profile, and they're the difference between a knife that becomes part of your kit and one that just looks good on a shelf.
  • So instead of a straight purchase button, we start with a conversation. Reach out, tell us what you're after, and we'll help you land on the piece that's actually right for you not just the one that's easiest to sell.
If you need a full record of how your data is handled for a specific order, contact us and we will review it with you in plain language.
