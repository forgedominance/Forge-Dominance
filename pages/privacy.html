Privacy Policy

We keep the data story simple: collect what is needed to process your order, keep it secure, and never treat your information like a product.

At a Glance

  • We only collect the details needed to fulfill a commission or answer your message.
  • We use website analytics to understand what pages people actually read.
  • Order and support data is retained so we can stand behind every build and warranty.

What We Collect

  • Contact information you submit through forms or direct email.
  • Order details, blade preferences, and shipment information.
  • Basic browsing analytics used to improve navigation and product pages.

How We Use It

  • To process commissions, send updates, and confirm delivery timelines.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, and repeated spam submissions.
  • To improve the site layout, content, and mobile experience.

Your Control

  • You can ask us to update or remove contact details that are no longer needed.
  • You can opt out of promotional messages at any time.
  • If you have a privacy question, we will answer it directly.
If you need a full record of how your data is handled for a specific order, contact us and we will review it with you in plain language.
