The Forge Dominance Difference

Why We Don't Offer Direct Checkout

Because a Blade This Serious
Deserves a Conversation.

Every Forge Dominance knife is built to order by one of three craftsmen. There is no warehouse. There is no inventory. There is only steel, fire, and the specific needs of the hunter who commissioned it.

Direct checkout assumes you know exactly what you need without asking where you're hunting, what you're dressing, and how you carry. We refuse to make that assumption.

When you contact us, James or Michael will personally discuss your use case, recommend steel and handle combinations you may not have considered, and ensure that the blade you receive is the blade you actually need — not just the one you clicked.
