AKTI Compliance

We make knives for lawful adult use and we support responsible ownership. This page explains our approach to compliance and common-sense handling.

Responsible Ownership Lawful Use Safety First

At a Glance

  • We sell products intended for adults who understand local laws.
  • We encourage safe storage, transport, and use in the field.
  • We will not help anyone bypass a law or policy.

Responsible Sales

  • We expect customers to know and follow the rules that apply where they live and travel.
  • Age-gate and legal notices are part of our normal customer flow.
  • If an order raises a legal concern, we pause and clarify before shipping.

Storage & Transport

  • Store blades securely and out of reach of children.
  • Transport knives in the manner required by the jurisdiction you are in.
  • Use sheaths, cases, and luggage rules that make sense for the trip.

Our Position

  • We are here to support lawful hunters, collectors, and working outdoors people.
  • We do not market to minors.
  • When laws are unclear, the safest answer is to verify first and carry second.
