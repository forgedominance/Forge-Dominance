Blade Laws by State

Knife laws change often. This page points you in the right direction, but local rules always win. Verify before you buy, carry, or travel.

Verify Local Law Travel Smart Know the Category

At a Glance

  • Blade length, concealment, and carry rules vary by jurisdiction.
  • Some places regulate fixed blades differently from folding knives.
  • When in doubt, use the most conservative carry option.

Before You Travel

  • Check the state, county, and city rules before carrying a fixed blade.
  • Air travel and public land rules can differ from everyday carry rules.
  • For hunting trips, verify the laws where you will actually be using the knife.

Common Differences

  • Some states distinguish between open carry and concealed carry.
  • Blade length limits may apply in schools, government buildings, and public events.
  • Sheath carry is not a universal exception.

Our Advice

  • Choose the blade that fits your use case, then choose the carry method that fits the law.
  • If you are unsure, ask local authorities or an attorney familiar with weapons law.
  • We would rather answer a laws question than lose you to a bad assumption.
This page is general guidance only and is not legal advice. You are responsible for following the laws where you live and where you travel.
