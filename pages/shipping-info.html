Shipping Info

We build to order, then ship with the same attention we use in the forge. Here is how timelines, tracking, and delivery expectations work.

Built to Order Tracking Provided Clear Timelines

International Shipping

  • We use DHL Express for shipping. It usually takes 4–5 days to reach your destination, depending on DHL's delivery schedule.
  • Import duties and taxes are not included unless stated.
    • Customs fees are the customer's responsibility.
    • Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by factors beyond our control, including customs clearance, adverse weather conditions, carrier delays, and other unforeseen circumstances. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Processing Time

  • Commission builds are processed after the design is confirmed and the deposit is received.
  • We will tell you the expected completion window before work begins.
  • If the build changes, we will update the timeline before moving forward.

Delivery Regions

  • We ship within the United States and to select international destinations where legal.
  • Adult signature or age verification may be required depending on the carrier.
  • Certain products may not be available for shipment to specific states or countries due to local laws and regulations. It is the customer's responsibility to ensure that purchasing and possessing our products is legal in their location.

Tracking & Customs

  • Tracking details are shared after the package leaves the shop.
  • Customs review or import duties are the customer’s responsibility where applicable.
  • If a shipment needs attention, we will work through it with you.
