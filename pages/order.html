    Why We Don't Offer Direct Checkout

    Because Your Blade
    Deserves a Conversation.

    Every Forge Dominance knife is built to order by one of three master craftsmen. There is no warehouse. There is no inventory. There is only steel, fire, and the specific needs of the hunter who commissioned it.
