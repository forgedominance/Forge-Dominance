    The Forge Dominance Difference

    Why We Don't Offer Direct Checkout

    Because Your Blade
    Deserves a Conversation.

    Every Forge Dominance knife is built to order by one of three master craftsmen. There is no warehouse. There is no inventory. There is only steel, fire, and the specific needs of the hunter who commissioned it.

    Direct checkout assumes you know exactly what you need without asking where you're hunting, what you're dressing, and how you carry. We refuse to make that assumption.

    When you contact us, our team discusses your use case, recommends steel and handle combinations you may not have considered, and ensures that the blade you receive is the blade you actually need.
