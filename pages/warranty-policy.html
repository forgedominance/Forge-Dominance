Warranty Policy

Our warranty is simple on purpose. If a knife fails because of a craftsmanship issue, we make it right. No maze, no fine print games.

Lifetime Coverage Craftsmanship First Original Owner

At a Glance

  • We stand behind material and workmanship for the life of the original owner.
  • Normal wear and misuse are reviewed case by case.
  • We prefer repair first when it keeps the blade in the field longer.

What Is Covered

  • Defects in materials or workmanship that appear during normal use.
  • Handle failures, assembly issues, or structural faults tied to build quality.
  • Repair or replacement options when the knife cannot be restored quickly.

How to Start a Claim

  • Send us clear photos, a short description, and the original order details if available.
  • We review the request and reply with the next step.
  • If the knife needs return service, we will explain packing and shipping instructions.

Common Limits

  • Heavy abuse, unauthorized modifications, and obvious misuse may not be covered.
  • Sharpening, finish wear, and normal patina are expected parts of ownership.
  • We will always look at the blade before we look for an excuse to say no.
