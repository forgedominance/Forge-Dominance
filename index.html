Precision Engineering

The Steel
Science

We obsess over metallurgy because you obsess over your hunt. Every material decision is engineered for field performance — not showroom appeal.

01

The Alloy

We match each blade to the steel it demands — from CPM powder-metallurgy steels used in aerospace and surgical instruments, to proven traditional alloys — selected for the knife's purpose, not a single one-size-fits-all spec.

CPM-154 · AUS-8 · Damascus
02

The Forge

Each blade is hand-forged at 2,300°F then triple-quenched. This aligns the molecular structure for maximum toughness — not just hardness. Impossible to replicate by machine.

2,300°F · Triple Quench
03

The Edge

Ground to a 15–17° per-side angle with a 0.001" tolerance — sharp enough to shave, tough enough for field use. Both, without compromise.

15–17° · ±0.001" Tolerance
04

The Hardness

Rockwell Hardness 58–62 HRC — well above the 50–56 HRC range typical of mass-produced blades. We test every single blade before it leaves our workshop. Every one. No exceptions.

58–62 HRC · Individual QC
05

The Handle

Vacuum-stabilized hardwood infused with resin at high pressure. Will not crack, warp, or absorb moisture — tested in conditions no competitor would attempt.

Vacuum Stabilized · Moisture-Proof
06

The Guarantee

No fine print. No "normal wear" exceptions. If your Forge Dominance knife ever fails to perform, we repair or replace it — for the life of the original owner. No limit. No fees. No arguments.

Lifetime · Unconditional

The Origin

14 Years.
One Standard.
No Compromise.

Before Forge Dominance existed, our founder spent 14 seasons guiding elk and deer hunts across the Rockies. He carried every major brand. He sharpened them. He fixed them. He threw most of them away.

In 2010 he picked up a hammer — not to start a business, but to solve a problem. The first blade took 60 hours. It still works perfectly today. That became our only standard.

We make fewer than 300 knives per year by design. Every single one leaves this workshop with two names on it — the craftsman who built it, and the hunter who will carry it.
